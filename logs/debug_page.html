    ScrumLaunch is a software development company supporting fast-growing US startups across various industries, with locations in Los Angeles, CA, and Portland, OR. We partner with startups by embedding product and engineering teams directly into their core operations.

     

    We’re looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Ruby on Rails + React) to join one of our clients, a US-based company that rents and sells premium furniture, mainly through rental portals and its own logistics application.

    The team is actively developing warehouse logistics, with a focus on optimizing intra-warehouse operations. The client keeps growing by acquiring new companies, and all its brands now run on one unified platform. The storefront has moved to Shopify, and the team supports an internal application used by employees and designers.

    The team includes three developers, two leads, a project manager, and a QA lead. The tech stack is React, TypeScript, NestJS, and Ruby on Rails.
     

    Requirements

    • 5+ years of professional software development experience.
    • Strong hands-on experience with Ruby on Rails, including building, maintaining, and optimizing production applications.
    • Strong experience with React.js, JavaScript (ES6+), and TypeScript.
    • Solid understanding of RESTful APIs, API design, and third-party integrations.
    • Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL, including database design, query optimization, indexing, and migrations.
    • Experience with background job processing (Sidekiq, Redis, or similar).
    • Solid understanding of software architecture, design patterns, SOLID principles, and clean code practices.
    • Experience writing automated tests (RSpec, Jest, React Testing Library, or similar).
    • Proficiency with Git, code reviews, CI/CD pipelines, and modern development workflows.
    • Strong understanding of authentication, authorization, and web application security.
    • Experience debugging complex issues and optimizing application performance.
    • Ability to work with existing codebases, including legacy systems and technical debt.
    • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to make independent technical decisions.
    • Readiness to work with a client in the US Eastern (EST) time zone.
    • English level: Upper-Intermediate or higher.
       

    Nice to have

    • Experience with NestJS.
    • Experience with Shopify integrations.
    • Experience with warehouse, logistics, or inventory management systems.
    • Experience with rental, booking, or e-commerce platforms.

    Cropwise Operations is a global leader in digital agritech solutions, operating in over 50 countries and helping optimize business processes and improve the efficiency of agronomic decision-making.  

    We are looking for a skilled Ruby on Rails Developer to join our team.

     

    What we expect from a candidate:

    - Preferably 3+ years of Ruby on Rails development experience.

    - Strong knowledge of Rails.

    - Experience with testing frameworks like RSpec and Capybara.

    - Testing with RSpec/Capybara.

    - Basic knowledge of ES6 + React.js.

    - Experience with JavaScript.

    - Standard Rails application deployment stack (Git, CI, Capistrano, *nix).

    - Knowledge of HTML/CSS.

     

    What exactly will you need to work with:

    - Backend on Ruby on Rails 7.0 (Ruby 3.2).

    - PostgreSQL + PostGIS database (handling large geospatial datasets).

    - Job processing with Resque + Redis.

    - Testing on RSpec + Capybara + CI.

    - Partially frontend on React.js (ES6, JSX).

     

    What else can you work with?

    - Algorithms for processing large amounts of geodata.

    - Reception and processing of binary data (Ruby eventmachine).

    - Working with RabbitMQ.

    - AWS, Google Cloud, Kubernetes.

    - Visualization and work with geodata on the frontend (Mapbox GL JS).

     

    Hiring process:

    - Intro call;

    - Test Task;

    - Final Interview;

    - Offer.

     

    The company offers:

    - Opportunity to be a part of a best-in-class product that is used by thousands of companies around the world.

    - Participation in complex technical tasks and challenges.

    - Environment where you need to seek and implement unique engineering and technical solutions.

    - Trainings and development programs.

    - Flexible work formats: remote, office or hybrid.

    - Health and life insurance for employees and your family members.

    - 32 working days of paid annual leave and sick leave.

     

    About Cropwise Operations

     

    Cropwise Operations is a SaaS platform for farmers, leveraging cutting-edge technologies such as computer vision, big data, and AI to deliver actionable insights and improve agricultural outcomes.

     

    For seekers, cooperation with Cropwise Operations is an opportunity to be part of a best-in-class product used by hundreds of companies around the world, and to deal with complex technical tasks in the field of neural networks and machine learning with the need to constantly improve their knowledge and skills, to find and implement unique engineering and technical solutions.
